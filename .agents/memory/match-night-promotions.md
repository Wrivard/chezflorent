---
name: Match-night promotion scope
description: Product decision separating permanent menu management from manually scheduled promotion dates.
---

The « Soir de match » menu category remains available for CMS editing and browsing; a promotion is announced only for match dates the owner manually adds to the agenda. Do not infer dates from an NHL/NFL feed or auto-create recurring events. Pricing and eligibility were deliberately left for the owner to enter in the CMS.

**Why:** The owner explicitly wanted control of which match dates count, and had not supplied confirmed prices or exact conditions for the two offer concepts.

**How to apply:** When extending match promotions, keep the menu content separate from the manually scheduled events; avoid publishing new prices, conditions, or game dates on the owner's behalf.
